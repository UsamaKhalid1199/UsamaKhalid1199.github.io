Bleedoku – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 (version 2.0.10)

Bleedoku is a colour-mixing puzzle game made by Game Studio ("we", "us"), an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (levels completed, stars, boosters, pets, settings, unfinished puzzles, daily painting and streaks) is saved only on your device. We do not run accounts and never upload your progress to our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bleedoku shows advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google and its partners may collect: your device's advertising identifier and App Set ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions (for example that an ad was shown or tapped). This information is shared with Google as our advertising provider. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google's consent service checks whether privacy choices are required before the game requests ads. Where required, including in the UK and the European Economic Area, a consent form is shown unless a valid choice has already been saved. Depending on your choices and availability, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or limited. Non-personalised or limited ads can still involve data processing, including fraud prevention. Where Google requires a privacy options entry point, you can revisit your choices in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Reward videos are always optional: the game only plays one when you tap a video button. Ads never appear in the middle of a level.

Gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To improve the game we measure how it is played: level starts and results, hints, optional reward videos, and how often players return. Google processes an app-instance identifier, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and session activity for this. Advertising identifiers and ad personalisation are switched off for analytics.

Gameplay analytics is disabled until Google's consent service reports that consent has been obtained or is not required. Where the European consent framework applies, the game checks your permission to store information on your device before enabling analytics. Refusing that permission disables gameplay analytics and resets locally stored analytics data. Where consent is not required, analytics can start without a form. The game does not provide a separate analytics switch. These choices do not prevent all processing by the advertising and consent services described above.

Retention and deletion

Your saved game remains on your device until you remove the app or its data. Google processes advertising and analytics data under its service terms and retention controls; retention depends on the data type and the configured service settings. See Google’s data retention information. Turning off analytics through the available consent choices stops future collection and resets analytics data stored by the SDK on your device; it does not itself delete data already sent to Google. Contact us below for a request concerning that data.

Diagnostics

The Google services above may collect crash and performance information about the app (for example, that the app closed unexpectedly). This information helps diagnose errors, improve service performance and prevent fraud.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No precise location.
  • No photos, files, microphone or camera access.
  • No purchases (the game has no in-app purchases).

Permissions

Permission Why
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Loading ads and sending gameplay analytics.
VIBRATE The optional Vibration setting.
AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION Used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads (subject to your consent choices).
BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE Lets Google Analytics know the app was installed from Google Play.
READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE Requested by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for basic device information; it does not read your phone number or calls.
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE Used by Google libraries to finish sending queued data in the background.

Your rights (UK and EEA)

We request your consent for personalised advertising and analytics where required. Advertising availability and the information used depend on your choices and Google’s consent requirements. Where consent choices are offered, you can revisit and withdraw them in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Email us to make a request. We do not hold player accounts, so we may need information to identify the relevant data; we will explain what is possible and how to use Google’s privacy controls. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Children

Bleedoku is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
