Bleedoku – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Bleedoku is a colour-mixing puzzle game published by Big O Soft LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels completed, stars, boosters, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run accounts, and we never upload your progress to our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Bleedoku shows advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type and app usage events (for example that an ad was shown or tapped). How Google uses this data is described in the Google privacy & terms.

In the UK and the European Economic Area the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.

Reward videos are always optional: the game never plays one unless you tap a "Watch a video" button.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No precise location.
  • No photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No purchases (the game is free and has no in-app purchases).

Permissions

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are used only to load advertisements. VIBRATE powers the optional "Gentle vibration" setting.

Children

Bleedoku is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
