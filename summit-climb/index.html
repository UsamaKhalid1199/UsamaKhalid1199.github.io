WHAT'S UNDER THE BONNET

Every run is a
race to the top.

Bikes, cars, trucks and monster trucks,
each with its own grip, weight and engine note.

  • REAL PHYSICS

    Real cars. Real suspension.

    16 vehicles in 4 classes, each with 4 tiers to unlock. Every upgrade, from engine and suspension to tyres and 4WD, is measurably better.

  • TEN STAGES

    From desert to the Moon.

    Broken bridges on Desert Highway, icy Frostbite Pass, Bogwater mud pits, lava rock on Ash Volcano, low gravity on the Moon and, finally, The Summit.

  • OUTRUN MODE

    They never get tired.

    Ram the walkers, chain combos and keep your fuel up. Wreck the car or let them catch you and the run is over.

  • THE ARMORY

    Ten pieces of kit.

    Machine gun, scattergun, flamethrower, rocket pod, ram bar, spiked plating, shock cage, nitro, mine dropper and armour plate.

  • YOU ARE THE DRIVER

    Your face behind the wheel.

    Pick a photo and your face rides in the driver's seat. The photo never leaves your phone.

  • EVERY DAY

    Missions and a league.

    A seven-day login streak, three new missions every day, and a league of thirty rival drivers to climb on coins, distance and zombies.
