Delete your Summit Climb data

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Summit Climb: Outrun the Horde (by Game Studio 05) has no accounts, so there is no account to close. Your game data lives on your phone, and the only data sent off your phone is the advertising and analytics data described in our privacy policy. Here is how to remove each kind.

Data on your phone

Your progress, coins, vehicles, upgrades, settings, purchase records and driver portrait are stored only in the game's private storage. To delete all of it, either:

  1. Uninstall the game: press and hold the Summit Climb icon and choose Uninstall, or go to Google Play > Summit Climb > Uninstall; or
  2. Clear its data: open Android Settings > Apps > Summit Climb > Storage > Clear storage (the exact names vary by phone).

This cannot be undone. Your progress is not backed up to our servers, so we cannot restore it.

Just your driver photo

To remove only your driver portrait, open the Garage in the game and tap REMOVE. The original photo was never kept or uploaded.

Ad and analytics identifiers

  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads). A deleted or reset ID can no longer be linked to past ad data.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): change or withdraw your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button on the main menu.
  • Analytics app instance ID: uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it; a new, unrelated ID is created if you install the game again.

Ask us to delete analytics data

To ask us to delete analytics data (Google Analytics for Firebase) already collected from your installation, email gamestudio05.team@gmail.com with the subject "Summit Climb data deletion request". Because we do not hold accounts, please include your phone model and roughly when you installed and last played the game, to help us find it. Do this before uninstalling if you can.

We will reply within 30 days and delete the matching data through Google's tools. Data we cannot match to your installation is not linked to you, and all analytics data is deleted automatically at the end of its retention period.
