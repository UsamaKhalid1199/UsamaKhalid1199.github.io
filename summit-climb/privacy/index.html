Summit Climb – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Summit Climb: Outrun the Horde is a single-player game published by Game Studio 05 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information on your device, and what our advertising and analytics provider, Google, collects.

Your driver photo

You can choose a photo from your gallery to appear as your driver. The game:

  • finds the face in the photo on your phone, using Android's built-in face detector;
  • keeps only a small 256 × 256 pixel portrait, in the game's private storage on your phone;
  • never uploads, shares or sends the photo or the portrait anywhere;
  • never keeps the original photo.

You can remove the portrait at any time in the Garage (REMOVE). Uninstalling the game also deletes it.

The game only sees the one photo you pick in the system photo picker; it does not request access to your photo library, camera or storage.

Game progress

Coins, unlocked vehicles, stages, upgrades, Armory items, records, daily rewards and settings are stored only on your phone, in the game's private storage. There are no accounts and we never upload your progress to our own servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • optional reward videos that you choose to watch (for example a full fuel tank, double coins or free coins);
  • occasionally, a full-screen ad between runs.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button on the main menu. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Ad requests are marked as not directed at children and limited to content suitable for teens.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to find and fix problems, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects:

  • app usage events (for example when you start or finish a run);
  • an app instance ID, a random identifier for this installation of the game;
  • device model and Android version, and app version;
  • approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address;
  • session data, such as when you open the game and how long you play.

This information is not linked to your name or to any account, and it never includes your driver photo or portrait. It is sent to and processed by Google on our behalf. See Privacy and security in Firebase and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Analytics data is kept for a limited period under Google Analytics' data-retention settings and then deleted automatically.

In-app purchases

The STORE sells coin packs, a Starter Pack and Remove Ads. Payments are made through Google Play Billing; the game never sees your card or payment details. The game keeps only a record of each purchase (the purchase token Google Play issues) on your phone, so that a purchase is delivered exactly once.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No precise location.
  • Your driver photo and portrait are never part of any ad request or analytics event.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores on your phone (progress, settings, purchase records and your driver portrait) is deleted when you uninstall the game, or when you clear its data in Android Settings > Apps > Summit Climb > Storage > Clear storage.

To reset the identifiers used for ads and analytics, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; reinstalling the game creates a new app instance ID. To ask us to delete analytics data already collected from your installation, email us. Full steps are on the delete your data page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the UK and the EEA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Email us to make a request. We do not hold player accounts, so we may need information (such as the date you installed the game and your phone model) to find the relevant data; we will explain what is possible and how to use Google's privacy controls. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It contains cartoon and realistic violence against zombies; blood can be switched off on the main menu. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds other online features such as cloud saves or leaderboards, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date before that version is released.

Contact
